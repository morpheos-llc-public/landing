Startups and Operators Pitch Night in NYC Manhattan

Wednesday, 11 Mar 2026 · 6:00–9:00 PM ET Whiskey Cellar NYC · East Village (Manhattan) $0–$45 · In-person · Pitch night + networking
I keep coming back to this one because it actually delivers. The Whiskey Cellar crew runs this monthly and they've dialed in the formula — 50 to 80 people max, real angel investors and VC partners in the room, and they'll hand you a pitch slot if you ask for one. Most pitch nights in this city are either too packed for a real conversation or too gatekept to get on stage. This one threads the needle. Show up at six, work the room before pitches kick off, and you'll walk out with warm intros you couldn't buy at a $500 conference.
